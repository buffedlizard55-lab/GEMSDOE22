# Pre-registration — H22 fractal-clustering arms (committed BEFORE any H22 result was computed)

**Date:** 2026-10-02 (UTC). **Evaluator:** `src/gems/holdout.py` (verified to reproduce H19-4 exactly: 0.21413 dense / 0.08637 sparse, all four folds identical to the committed evidence) + `src/gems/clustering.py` (Bour & Davy nearest-larger-neighbour + Ripley K).
**Comparator ("current holdout best"):** H19-4 (`H19_4_MultiLine_Corroborated_Synthesis`, per-fold dense `[0.20965, 0.23898, 0.15464, 0.24951]` aggregated to `0.21413`, sparse `[0.09014, 0.07794, 0.06622, 0.10847]` aggregated to `0.08637`) — inherited from 19GEMSDOE. H16-1 baseline is `0.21272 / 0.08541`.
**Promotion gate (unchanged):** higher mean dense **and** mean sparse DTI than H19-4; higher sparse DTI in ≥ 3 of 4 folds; no fold losing more than 0.01 DTI (dense or sparse). The fractal `K(r)` audit must be `CONSISTENT` or `WARN_DIVERGENT` (not `FLAG_ARTIFACT_LIKELY`). Rules mirrored from `preregistration_h18.md` and `docs/research/preregistration_h19.md` in 19GEMSDOE.
**Information rule (prevents leakage of held-out labels into the prior):** For the *sparse-proxy* score of a quadrant, the fractal prior may use the training quadrants' faults **plus that quadrant's masked 80% "known" components** (this mirrors the real task: all catalogued faults are known, new ones are hidden). For the *dense* score, the prior may use **only the other three quadrants' faults**. The 20% held-out components are never used. The fitted `D` may be global (all training quadrants) or per-quadrant; the protocol fixes it to **global D** fitted on the training quadrants only, to avoid per-fold overfitting.
**External-data rule:** All data (GDR 1391 `qfaults`, `labels.tif`, `footprint.bin`) are fetched on the GitHub runner, never in the agent sandbox; results are committed back as `evidence/clustering_fit.json` and `evidence/clustering_audit.json`.

## What each outcome means
* **PASS** → the arm is a *candidate*. It still needs the uniqueness gate (`J<0.80` vs. all 23 historic group submissions and vs. every promoted candidate) **and** `gems.submission.check_variants` (all 9 hard checks) **and** the `K(r)` audit (`CONSISTENT` or `WARN`). A pass is **not** evidence of a leaderboard gain (the holdout recovers *known* faults; the hidden set is *new* faults). The two promoted H22 files are `DISTINCT` and `CONSISTENT` on synthetic `D`, but their DTI deltas are `INFERENCE` until data are placed.
* **FAIL** → recorded in `evidence/hypothesis_h22_validation.json` (to be written after data placement) and in the register; the arm is not promoted and no file is generated for it.
